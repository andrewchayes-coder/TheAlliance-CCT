---
name: Staff profile privacy
description: Staff roster purpose, county ownership and private-field audit restrictions.
---
Staff need volunteer details at a glance in the roster, not only on individual detail pages.

**Why:** Client feedback: "To view most volunteer details, admins have to go into individual volunteer contacts rather than in a table."

**How to apply:** Keep staff contact/profile visibility and column preferences on the roster when extending it. County is staff-managed (default Placer), not a volunteer onboarding question or volunteer-editable field.

Private profile edit activity must record field names only, never their values.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires this for birth date, address, emergency contact, county and start-date edits.

**How to apply:** Keep values out of new activity records. Existing historical values are not rewritten automatically; sanitize them from activity responses and rendering.
