---
name: CCT scheduling policy
description: Client-confirmed operating grid and how date blocks and one-time shifts relate to standing commitments.
---
The client confirmed Monday–Friday, 9 AM–6 PM, with two-hour shifts starting each hour from 9 AM through 4 PM, capacity two. Overlap is intentional; never “fix” it by making the grid nonoverlapping. Volunteers themselves cannot hold overlapping commitments.

**Why:** This is the confirmed Community Care Team operating schedule, and dispatch needs to distinguish both live shifts at overlapping times.

One-time shifts do not count toward the two standing-shift cap. Date-specific blocks suppress operations for that occurrence without ending the underlying weekly assignment. Staff capacity overrides require explicit consent and a recorded reason, never a volunteer bypass.

**Why:** The client distinguishes regular weekly commitments from special events and temporary staff-training/partial-day closures.

Volunteers can choose one-time or recurring bookings on the regular grid, not only claim staff-created custom shifts.

**Why:** The user requested a frequency choice during volunteer sign-up.

**How to apply:** Preserve these distinctions in availability, dispatch, coverage, reminders, exports and cancellation confirmations. Restoring a week off or blocked occurrence must not silently double-book a one-time reservation.
