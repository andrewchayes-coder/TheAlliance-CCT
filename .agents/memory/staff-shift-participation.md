---
name: Staff shift participation
description: Staff scheduling eligibility is separate from volunteer onboarding and role access.
---

Admins take shifts using their existing accounts and the admin schedule assignment controls. Staff scheduling does not require a second volunteer login or fabricated volunteer onboarding details.

**Why:** admins need to cover shifts while retaining administrative access. Expanding volunteer-only access globally would weaken the existing role boundary.

**How to apply:** keep the staff eligibility exception scoped to scheduling. Preserve normal volunteer onboarding checks, keep staff out of volunteer roster/attention counts, and release staff assignments when their account is deactivated.
