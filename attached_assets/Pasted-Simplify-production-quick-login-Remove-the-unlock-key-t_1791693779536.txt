Simplify production quick login. Remove the unlock key, the /qa unlock link, and the device cookie. Quick login becomes a single on/off switch.

ENABLE RULE
- Dev (NODE_ENV !== 'production'): always on (unchanged).
- Production: on when QUICK_LOGIN_ENABLED, trimmed and lowercased, equals "true". Otherwise off. Nothing else is required.
- When on: the quick-login card shows on /signin for anyone, and GET /api/auth/quick-login/status and POST /api/auth/quick-login work with no cookie. When off: both return 404 and /signin shows no card (unchanged).

REMOVE
- QUICK_LOGIN_KEY: stop reading it anywhere. Remove the 32-character rule, the startup warning about a missing or short key (quickLoginConfigurationWarning and its call in server/startup-check.ts), and the key redaction in the quick-login audit log's user agent.
- The /qa and /qa/lock routes (qaUnlockRouter and its app.use in app.ts), the qa_unlock cookie, and everything that creates, signs, or checks it (createQaUnlockCookie, validQaUnlockCookie, matchesQuickLoginKey, the signature helper, QA_COOKIE, QA_LIFETIME, qaCookieOptions), plus the /qa rate limit. /qa and /qa/lock just get the app's normal not-found page.
- hasQuickLoginAccess(req) becomes isQuickLoginEnabled() (or call isQuickLoginEnabled directly in routes/quick-login.ts and auth/access-policy.ts).
- The "Hide test logins on this device" link in components/quick-login.tsx. Keep the PRODUCTION pill.

KEEP (don't loosen these)
- POST signs in only users with isTestAccount = true, isActive = true, the persona's role, and a verified email. A real account can never be reached.
- CSRF/origin check, session regeneration, the 30/hour POST rate limit, the audit entries (activity_log + app log), and the public:quick-login route-guard tag.
- Test-account exclusions, email suppression, TEST badges, the banner with Switch account, and Reset test accounts. ensureTestAccounts still runs at startup whenever quick login is on.
